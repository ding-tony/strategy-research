## Method and validation

- 2022-02-07 through 2026-10-02, $3000, 3 stocks, monthly full weight maintenance, sector count cap 2, retention buffer 1, $100 ordinary adjustments minimum, 25% stock stop, 85%-initial-capital portfolio floor. No earnings exits or 50% concentration trigger.
- Conservative investment-plus-momentum monthly ranking only; this is not the skip-month strategy.
- Monthly dates are the archived first weekly review session in each month, not always the exact first trading day. Sector cap limits stock count, not sector dollar weight.
- Paired stop comparisons freeze each no-stop target schedule, matching the earlier monthly stop study. This isolates execution; it does not rerank retention after stop exits.
- 25% close-confirmed stop uses production implementation; next-session-open full exit, cash until monthly review. Fixed 85% starting-capital floor is daily-close-to-next-open; no interest.
- Synthetic dividend-adjusted prices and previously recorded monthly fills, no fees/spread/slippage/FX/tax. Current cached universe, not complete dated membership: survivorship and incomplete fundamental-availability biases. Previously examined in-sample period, not independent validation.
- Sharpe uses sample daily arithmetic returns, zero risk-free rate, sqrt(252). Independently reconciled cash/NAV and episode P&L; prior baseline and stop study reproduced. All active labs unchanged.
- This comparison changes only monthly rank weights: 50/33.333/16.667 versus 40/40/20, both with existing 25% stop. Uses production allocation and stop implementation; research-only instance weight override, not configuration change.
- Same conservative monthly target baskets and dates. Stops do not cause selection reranking. $100 ordinary minimum can skip small adjustments, so actual weights need not exactly match targets.
- First buys verified: $1500/$1000/$500 versus $1200/$1200/$600. Baseline every trade and metrics reproduce prior study; independently rebuilt cash/NAV and profit. Active labs unchanged.
- Annual returns are sequential calendar-year portfolio returns, with partial 2022 and 2026 endpoints. These examined years are descriptive, not independent validation; selecting weights after reviewing rank results risks overfitting.
